Color Management

One pipeline,
from set to master

A decision made on set drifts a little as the material moves through edit and VFX. Preventing that drift is half of this job.

ACES is the working reference. The gamut the camera captured stays wide all the way through, and gets mapped only at the moment the deliverable is defined. An SDR grade never has to be rebuilt from scratch for HDR.

The on-set look travels forward as AMF, CDL and LUT. When the edit suite and VFX see what I see, nothing has to be found again at the end.

Some projects run in native camera space instead of ACES. Either way, a pipeline document is written and circulated to every department before the first day of shooting.

Review

Review with one link

Work in progress goes out as a link. It opens in a browser — no install, no login.

Notes go directly on the picture, carrying the timecode of the frame you paused on. No more hunting for "somewhere around three minutes, a face looks red." Several people can comment without their notes colliding, and an approve button closes the round.
